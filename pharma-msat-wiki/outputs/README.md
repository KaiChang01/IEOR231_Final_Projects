# outputs/ — Query Results and Derived Content

This directory stores everything generated from wiki queries: answers rendered as documents, slide decks, diagrams, and so on.

## What gets saved here

- `YYYY-MM-DD_query-slug.md` — answers to ad-hoc questions researched against the wiki
- `YYYY-MM-DD_slides_topic.md` — Marp-format slide decks (viewable in Obsidian with the Marp plugin)
- `YYYY-MM-DD_diagram_topic.svg` — process flow or org charts
- `YYYY-MM-DD_comparison_topic.md` — structured comparisons across wiki articles

## The feedback loop

Good outputs should be filed back into `wiki/` to enrich it. For example, if you ask a complex question about campaign planning and get a detailed answer, that answer can become a new wiki article or an addendum to an existing one.

To file an output back:
> "File `outputs/2026-05-04_query-tech-transfer-checklist.md` into the wiki as a new article under tech_transfer/."
